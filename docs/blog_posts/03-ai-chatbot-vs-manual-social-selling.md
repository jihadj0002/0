## Why Old-School Chatbots Failed

Let's be honest. Many sellers tried chatbots before and had bad experiences.

### The Problem with Rule-Based Chatbots

Tools like ManyChat, Chatfuel, and Jadubot use keyword matching. They work like this:

> Customer types: "abutine er price koto?"
> Bot sees: no keyword match for "abutine"
> Bot replies: "Sorry, I didn't understand"

**Bangla misspellings destroy rule-based bots.** Customers don't type perfectly. They type "abutine" instead of "arbutin cream." They mix Bengali and English. They use slang.

Rule-based bots can't handle this. Customers get frustrated. They leave. The seller thinks "chatbots don't work."
