## 3 Ways Social sellers Handle This (And Why They Fail)

### Method 1: Hire a Moderator
A full-time moderator costs BDT 10,000-15,000/month. They work 8 hours, not 24. They get sick. They quit. And they still can't reply to 100+ messages instantly.

### Method 2: Use Facebook's Quick Replies
Facebook's built-in auto-reply sends "Thanks for your message, we'll get back to you." Customers hate this. They want answers, not acknowledgments. Auto-replies actually lower trust.

### Method 3: Copy-Paste Saved Replies
You keep a notepad with prices. You copy-paste. It's faster — but it still takes time. And you can't do this at 2 AM when a customer wants to order.
