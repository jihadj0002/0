If you're a business owner in Bangladesh, you've probably heard the term "F-Commerce." Maybe you're already doing it without knowing the name. You have a Facebook page, customers message you, you take orders through Messenger, and payment happens via bKash or COD.

That's F-Commerce. And it's bigger than you think.

## What is F-Commerce?

F-Commerce (Facebook Commerce) is the practice of buying and selling products directly through Facebook — primarily through Facebook Pages and Messenger. No website. No e-commerce platform. Just a Facebook page, product photos, and customer conversations.

In Bangladesh, F-Commerce isn't just a trend — **it's the backbone of online selling.**

## How Big is F-Commerce in Bangladesh?

- **50M+** Facebook users in Bangladesh
- **Millions** of transactions happen daily through Facebook Messenger
- **Thousands** of businesses operate exclusively through Facebook Pages
- Categories: Clothing, beauty products, electronics, handmade goods, food, baby products

Unlike Western markets where e-commerce means Shopify or Amazon, in Bangladesh, **the chat is the store.**

## How F-Commerce Works (Step by Step)

### For the Customer:
1. They see your product on your Facebook Page or in a Facebook Group
2. They send a message: "Price koto?" or "Available ase?"
3. You reply with price, photos, and details
4. They confirm: "Ami order kortey chai"
5. You collect name, address, phone number
6. Payment: bKash/Nagad or COD (Cash on Delivery)
7. You send the product via courier (Steadfast, Pathao, Sundarban, Redx)

### For the Seller:
1. Post product photos on your Facebook Page
2. Reply to customer messages manually (or use automation)
3. Track orders in notebooks or spreadsheets
4. Coordinate with courier services
5. Follow up with customers after delivery

## The Problem with F-Commerce in 2026

F-Commerce is growing fast. But most sellers are still operating like it's 2018.

### The 3 Biggest Challenges:

**1. Message Overload**
A page with 10,000 followers can easily get 100+ messages daily. Replying to each one takes hours. Most sellers can't keep up.

**2. No Central System**
Orders are scattered across Messenger, Instagram DMs, and WhatsApp. Tracking who ordered what becomes impossible. Orders get lost. Customers get frustrated.

**3. Late Replies = Lost Sales**
If a customer messages at 11 PM and you reply at 9 AM, they've already bought from someone else. In F-Commerce, speed = sales.
