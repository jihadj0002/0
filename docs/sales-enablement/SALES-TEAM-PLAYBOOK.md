# Sales Team Playbook

**Purpose**: Hire, onboard, and manage a commission-only sales team to find clients and feed the CRM pipeline.
**Goal**: 10 paying customers by Aug 15, 2026 → 50 by Sep 30, 2026.
**Created**: 2026-08-03 | **Status**: Draft — pending Jihad approval

---

## 1. Team Structure

```
Jihad (Owner)
 ├── Approves pricing/offers, closes HOT leads (🟢), pays commissions
 ├── Sales Agent 1 — owns Tier 1 (high-priority) + HOT leads follow-up
 ├── Sales Agent 2 — owns Tier 2 (5-10K followers)
 └── Sales Agent 3 — owns Tier 3 + new prospecting (5 new/day)
```

| Phase | When | Team | Why |
|-------|------|------|-----|
| Phase 1 | Aug 3-10 | 2 agents | Validate that commission-only works, test the playbook |
| Phase 2 | Aug 11+ | 3-5 agents | Scale once 1 agent closes 2+ customers |
| Phase 3 | Sep | 5-10 agents | Push to 50 customers |

**Start small.** Hire 2 agents first. Only scale after one of them closes 2+ customers.

## 2. Compensation Plan (Commission-Only)

### Base Commission — 25% of first month fee

| Plan | Monthly Fee | Commission per Closed Customer |
|------|-------------|-------------------------------|
| Basic 999 | BDT 999 | BDT 250 |
| Basic 2499 | BDT 2,499 | BDT 625 |
| Pro | BDT 4,999 | BDT 1,250 |
| Enterprise | Quote | 25% of first month |

### Bonuses

| Bonus | Amount | Condition |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| Fast-close | +5% (total 30%) | Customer pays within 48h of demo |
| Weekly top closer | BDT 500 | Most customers closed in the week (min 2) |
| First-customer boost | BDT 250 extra | On their first-ever closed customer |
| Volume tier | +2% per month | 5+ customers closed in a single month |

### Payment Rules (anti-gaming)

1. Commission paid **after customer's first payment is collected** AND customer stays active 7 days.
2. Customer must be marked ✅ in `PIPELINE.md` and logged in `CONTACT-LOG.md` by the agent.
3. No commission on customers you sourced before hiring (HOT leads 🟢 belong to Jihad until delegated).
4. Lead ownership: **first toucher owns the lead**. No poaching. Disputes → Jihad decides.
5. Commissions settled every Sunday by midnight (e-payment: bKash/Nagad).

### What the agent must do to get paid

- Log every touch in `CONTACT-LOG.md` (template in section 5)
- Keep their owned rows in `PIPELINE.md` updated
- Attend weekly call
- Never promise price below the plan table — discounts only via Jihad

## 5. CRM Rules (non-negotiable)

### Every touch = log it. Same day. No exceptions.

Add a new row at the top of the table in `../03_SALES/crm/CONTACT-LOG.md`:

```markdown
| Date | Business | Channel | Type | Message / Summary | Response | Next Action |
|------|----------|---------|------|-------------------|----------|-------------|
| 2026-08-03 | [Name] | Call/FB DM/WhatsApp | Pitch/Demo/Follow-up | [1 line, e.g. "Called, introduced MatrixAI"] | [1 line, e.g. "Owner busy, asked to call Sat 11am"] | [Specific: "Call Sat 11:00 — Day-1 script"] |
```

Then update their row in `PIPELINE.md`:
- Move to correct stage section
- Update `Last Touch` + `Next Action`

### Lead ownership

| Lead type | Who owns |
|-----------|----------|
| 🔵 New (Tier 1-3) | Assigned agent |
| 🟡 Active conversation | The agent who started it |
| 🟢 HOT leads | **Jihad only** — agents report, Jihad closes |
| ✅ Lost | Recycle after 30 days via fresh campaign |

### Cadence rules (from `../03_SALES/crm/CADENCE.md`)

- First touch Day 1 → Follow-up Day 3 → Day 7 → Day 14 break-up → Lost
- No agent touches a lead more than 5 times total
- Agents may NOT discount price — only Jihad approves offers

### Quality checks (Jihad, every Sunday)

- [ ] Every agent logged ≥ 20 touches this week
- [ ] Pipeline stage counts match CONTACT-LOG
- [ ] HOT leads contacted same day
- [ ] No lost leads without 3+ documented touches

## 6. Onboarding (30 min call + 7-day training)

1. **Product (10 min)**: Walk through `00_CONTEXT/features_bengali.md` + `pricing.md`. Agent must be able to answer: "কত টাকা?" (999/2499/4999) and "কী কী করে?" (instant reply, product দেখায়, অর্ডার নেয়).
2. **Scripts (10 min)**: Role-play the Day-1 call and FB DM from `../03_SALES/crm/CADENCE.md` twice — agent plays seller, you play customer.
3. **CRM (10 min)**: Show them `PIPELINE.md` + `CONTACT-LOG.md`, have them log one practice touch.
4. **Homework (before day 1)**:
   - Call 3 businesses from their list, log all 3 touches
   - Send you a voice note reciting: "আমি MatrixAI থেকে বলছি — আমরা সোশ্যাল সেলারদের জন্য AI চ্যাটবট বানাই..."
   - Join WhatsApp sales group

### Training curriculum (required before first live call)

Every new agent studies `training/TRAINING-INDEX.md` — 10 modules, one per day. **No module 7 quiz pass (80%), no first live call.** Modules 8-9 must be read before the agent's second week live; **module 10 (how to use the CRM app) must be read before the first live day.** Jihad checks progress in the Monday call.

| Day | Module | Check by Jihad |
|-----|--------|----------------|
| 1 | `training/module-01-product.md` | Agent recites one-sentence pitch |
| 2 | `training/module-02-prospecting.md` | Brings 5 real leads |
| 3 | `training/module-03-first-contact.md` | Says call opening from memory |
| 4 | `training/module-04-objection-handling.md` | Handles 3 objections in roleplay |
| 5 | `training/module-05-demo-and-close.md` | Runs 10-min demo flow |
| 6 | `training/module-06-crm-discipline.md` | Logs 3 practice touches |
| 7 | `training/module-07-roleplay-and-quiz.md` | Passes quiz ≥ 16/20 |
| 8 | `training/module-08-leads-and-qualification.md` | Scores 5 leads Hot/Warm/Cold + explains jackpot signal |
| 9 | `training/module-09-sales-psychology.md` | Uses loss-framing + anchoring in roleplay |
| 10 | `training/module-10-crm-operations.md` | Logs a real lead, call + follow-up in the app live |

## 7. Weekly Operating Rhythm

| Day | Time | Activity |
|-----|------|----------|
| Mon | 10:00 | 30-min call: review pipeline, HOT leads, agent blockers |
| Daily | — | Agents: 5 new touches minimum (2 calls + 3 DMs) |
| Fri | 18:00 | Agents submit weekly summary: touches, replies, demos, blockers |
| Sun | — | Jihad: quality check (section 5), pay commissions, update `GROWTH.md` |

### Weekly targets per agent

| Metric | Target | Below → Action |
|--------|--------|----------------|

| Closed customers | 2+/week (Phase 2+) | Review objections, adjust offer |

## 9. What To Do When...

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Agent not logging touches | Warning 1 → remove after 3rd miss (they still earn earned commissions) |
| Agent spamming same script to wrong businesses | Retrain; re-check their lead list assignments |
| HOT lead goes cold | Jihad personally re-engages with the 48h stalled script |
| Agent closes a customer who churns in 7 days | No commission (per rule 1), debrief what went wrong |
| 0 closings after 2 weeks by all agents | Stop hiring, fix offer/script/demo video, then rehire |
