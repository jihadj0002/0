# Module 7 — Roleplay & Quiz

**Goal**: Pass this before your first live call (80% = 16/20). Then practice the 5 roleplays with a teammate. This turns reading into selling.

---

## Part 1 — The 20-Question Quiz

**Section A: Product & Pricing (Q1-5)**
1. How many plans are there, and what's the starting price?
2. Name 3 things MatrixAI does for a customer automatically.
3. What's the #1 business pain MatrixAI solves?
4. "বট কি আমার কোনা প্রোডাক্ট বুঝবে?" — correct answer?
5. Can you agree to a discount on the call? What do you say instead?

**Section B: Prospecting (Q6-9)**
6. Name 3 signs a business is a high-value prospect.
7. How long should you chase a lead with no phone number before skipping?
8. What's the "jackpot" comment section sign?
9. Name 1 source type for finding new leads.

**Section C: First Contact (Q10-13)**
10. What's the FIRST thing you say after greeting? Why?
11. Name 2 pain questions.
12. After they describe their problem, what must you do? (optional but wrong to skip)
13. When setting a demo, how many times do you offer and how? (two concrete...)

**Section D: Objections (Q14-16)**
14. What are the 3 steps of the objection response framework?
15. "আমরা ঠিক আছি" — what's the real concern and one comeback?
16. After 2 objections in a row, what do you do?

**Section E: Demo, CRM & Rules (Q17-20)**
17. What do you do BEFORE collecting payment?
18. Who approves discounts and owns hot leads?
19. How long must a customer stay active before your commission is paid?
20. When do you write to CONTACT-LOG — and what happens if you don't?

**Section F: Leads & Psychology — BONUS (Q21-24, from modules 8-9)**
21. What 3 things make a lead a lead? (যোগাযোগ + সমস্যা + সম্ভাবনা)
22. What's the jackpot signal — and why does it close more than any feature pitch?
23. Name the 6 persuasion principles and the ONE Bengali line you'd use for loss aversion.
24. After asking the closing question, what do you do? (বলবেন না — চুপ থাকুন)

> **Answer key**: All answers are in Modules 1-6 (sections A-E) and Modules 8-9 (section F). If you can't answer one in under 5 seconds, re-read that module before your first call.

## Part 2 — Roleplay Scenarios (practice with a friend/teammate)

### Scenario 1 — The Busy Owner (goal: book the demo)
> You call a cosmetics page. Owner answers, tense, says "এখন ভীষণ ব্যস্ত, পরে বলুন।"

Your move: acknowledge, ask for 45 seconds, hit the pain, offer two demo times.

---

### Scenario 2 — The Price Fighter
> Owner: "৯৯৯ টাকা? বন্ধুর ওয়েবসাইট ৫০০ টাকায় বানিয়ে দিয়েছে।"
Your move: acknowledge, redirect to value (daily cost, lost sales), back to demo. No discount.

---

### Scenario 3 — The Skeptic
> Owner: "বট দিয়ে মানুষ ভরসা করবে না, নিজে কথা বলা লাগে।"
Your move: acknowledge, name the reality (fast reply = more sales), show human handoff feature, offer free demo.

---

### Scenario 4 — The "Marcus" (multiple objections, stop the quiz show)
> Owner throws: "বেশি দাম... AI মনে হয় কাজ করবে না... মালিকের সাথে কথা বলুন..."
Your move: after the 2nd objection, STOP defending, book the demo: "এসব ডেমোতে দেখাই — ১০ মিনিট।"

---

### Scenario 5 — The Hot Lead Handoff
> You called a 40K-follower page. Owner asks: "কেন যাচ্ছি? আগ্রহী। নম্বর দিন।" Then says "শেষে বলবেন কিছু, আপডেট নিন।"
Your move: respond calmly, log instantly (🟢), report to Jihad in the group WITHIN the hour. Don't close it yourself.

## Part 3 — The Simulated Live Week (game)

Run this in one sitting with a partner who plays prospects:

| Day | Your task | Partner plays |
|-----|-----------|---------------|
| Mon | 5 touches (2 calls, 3 DMs) | 2 pick up, 3 ignore |
| Tue | Follow-ups Day 3 | 1 replies, 1 asks price |
| Wed | Demo 1 | Asks "বট আমার বাংলা বুঝবে?" |
| Thu | Handle 2 objections | Price fighter + skeptic |
| Fri | Close + log | 1 says yes → you log ✅ |
| Sun | Submit weekly report | — |

If you get through all 7 days without skipping a step or saying "না, আপডেতা নেই" — you're ready to sell for real.
