# Module 8 — Leads & Qualification Mastery

**Goal**: Understand what a lead is, how to score one in 2 minutes, what stage to put it in, and when to hand it to Jihad. This is the skill that separates closers from order-takers.
**Built with**: prospecting skill (ICP, Hot/Warm/Cold scoring) + revops skill (lead lifecycle, handoff SLA)

---

## 1. What Is a Lead? (সহজ ভাষায়)

> **লিড (Lead)** = এমন একটা ব্যবসা/মানুষ যার সমস্যা MatrixAI সমাধান করতে পারে, আর যার কাছে আমরা যোগাযোগ করার সুযোগ পেয়েছি।

A lead is NOT:
- ❌ Just a phone number in your list (that's a contact)
- ❌ Someone who liked your message (that's an engagement)
- ❌ A closed customer (that's a customer — different stage)

**Lead = যোগাযোগ + সমস্যা + সম্ভাবনা** — three things together. No problem, no lead.

## 2. The Lead Lifecycle (কীভাবে লিড বড় হয়)

```
🔵 New → 🟡 Conversation → 🟢 Interested → ✅ Customer
   (নতুন)      (কথা চলছে)      (আগ্রহী)        (পেমেন্ট)

        🔴 Stalled ← কোনো উত্তর নেই
        ❌ Lost → 30 দিন পর আবার রিসাইকেল
```

| Stage | Emoji | What must happen | Owner |
|-------|-------|------------------|-------|
| New | 🔵 | First contact — call/DM today | Agent |
| In Conversation | 🟡 | Active chat — keep reply < 24h | Agent |
| Interested | 🟢 | Asked price/asked for number/wants demo | **Jihad closes** |
| Stalled | 🔴 | 48h no reply — re-engage script | Agent |
| Converted | ✅ | Payment received + account active | Jihad + Agent setup |
| Lost | ❌ | 3+ touches, no response — recycle in 30d | Agent |

**Most important rule**: a lead can only move ONE stage per meaningful interaction. Never jump 🔵 → ✅ on one message. That's how deals fall apart.

## 3. The 2-Minute Lead Score (প্রতিটা লিডের আগে)

From the prospecting skill: score every lead **Hot / Warm / Cold** before you spend time.

| Question | Hot (✅) | Warm (🟡) | Cold (❌) |
|----------|---------|-----------|-----------|
| 1. Page active? (post in last 7 days) | Yes | Last 30 days | 60+ days silent |
| 2. Sells products on Messenger? | Takes orders in DM | Sells via website/app | Unclear |
| 3. Replies to comments? | Replies to all | Sometimes | Never replies |
| 4. Has the exact pain? | Complains about slow replies | Missing messages visible | No complaints visible |
| 5. Can we reach the owner? | Phone number + answers | Number, no answer yet | No number at all |

**Scoring rule**: 
- **4-5 ✅ = HOT** → call TODAY, work it like it's the only lead you have
- **3 ✅ = WARM** → call + follow the cadence (Day 3, Day 7, Day 14)
- **0-2 ✅ = COLD** → one touch only, then move on. Don't fall in love.

> **"Hot" needs evidence.** From the skill: a hot lead without a buying signal isn't hot — it's a wish. The signal = they've SHOWN they have the problem (visible missed "মূল্য?" comments, slow reply patterns, "আমরা পারি না" admissions).

## 4. The Jackpot Signal (গোপন অস্ত্র)

> **একটা পেজের পোস্টে ১০+ জন মন্তব্য করেছে "মূল্য?", "কত টাকা?", "ডেলিভারি?" — এবং কোনো উত্তর পায়নি।**

That's NOT a broken page — that's 10+ **provable lost sales**. Call that owner with:
> "আপনার গত পোস্টে ২০ জন দাম জিজ্ঞেস করেছে — কেউ উত্তর পায়নি। আমরা এটা থামাতে পারি।"

This one line closes more than any feature pitch. Because it's THEIR loss, in THEIR page, TODAY.

## 5. Qualification Questions (মালিকের সাথে কথা হলে — এই ৫টা জিজ্ঞেস করুন)

These five questions tell you in 3 minutes whether to push or pass:

| # | Question | What it tells you | If good |
|---|----------|-------------------|---------|
| 1 | "একদিনে কয়টা মেসেজ আসে?" | Volume → need | 20+ = hot |
| 2 | "উত্তর দিতে কতক্ষণ লাগে?" | Pain severity | "ঘণ্টা ঘণ্টা" = hot |
| 3 | "রাতে কে উত্তর দেয়?" | 24/7 gap | "কেউ না" = hot |
| 4 | "কাস্টমার চলে গেছে কখনো?" | Acknowledged loss | "হ্যাঁ" = ready to buy |
| 5 | "কী চাইলে আজই শুরু করতেন?" | Decision criteria | Price/setup/time = closable |

**Listen 80%, talk 20%.** If you talked more than they did, you lost the sale.

## 6. Speed-to-Lead = Sales (দ্রুত যোগাযোগের মূল্য)

From the revops skill — handoff SLA. Simplified for us:

| Situation | Contact within |
|-----------|----------------|
| 🟢 Interested lead reported to Jihad | **Same hour** |
| New lead assigned to you | **Same day** (call) |
| Lead replied to your DM | **Within 1 hour** (while they're online!) |
| Stalled lead re-engagement | Same day they were flagged |

> **Why speed wins**: a customer who just asked "কত টাকা?" on Facebook is shopping RIGHT NOW. In 3 hours they'll be talking to someone else. Speed is free and it beats every feature we have.

## 7. Lead Hygiene (লিড-লিস্ট পরিষ্কার রাখা)

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| Log every touch same day | No log = no commission + no proof |
| Never delete a lead — mark ❌ Lost | The list is data; lost leads get recycled |
| No duplicates — check CONTACT-LOG first | Two agents calling the same page = embarrassment |
| After 5 touches with no reply → ❌ | Your time > ghosts |
| After 30 days → recycle with new angle | New campaign, new message, fresh start |

**Pipeline math (know your funnel):**

```
25 touches (1 week)  →  10 conversations  →  3 demos  →  1 closed customer
```

If you closed 0 this week → check where the funnel leaks. 25 touches? 10 convos? This is the math that pays your commission.

## 8. When to Hand to Jihad (হ্যান্ডঅফ নিয়ম)

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Asked for phone number | Log 🟢 + tell Jihad same hour |
| "দাম কত?" twice | Log 🟢 + tell Jihad |
| "ডেমো দেখাই" | You can show the demo, but closing + payment = Jihad |
| Wants discount | "দাম আমরা ঠিক রেখেছি — অফার আছে, মালিককে জিজ্ঞেস করি" → tell Jihad |
| Paid customer | ✅ log + setup + welcome message |

**Golden rule**: You find and warm the lead. Jihad closes. That's the system that pays you 25%.
