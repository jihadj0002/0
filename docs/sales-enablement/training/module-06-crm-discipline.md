# Module 6 — CRM Discipline

**Goal**: Keep the pipeline clean and honest. The CRM is your money — no log, no commission. This module teaches exactly how to use it.

---

## 1. Why CRM Discipline Pays You

- Every logged touch = proof of work = commission safety.
- Jihad sees your activity → decides bonuses (weekly top closer = BDT 500).
- A clean pipeline means you never forget a hot lead.
- **Rule**: A lead with no log is a lead you didn't touch.

---

## 2. The Two Files You Use

| File | What It Is | When You Update It |
|------|-----------|--------------------|
| `crm/PIPELINE.md` | All leads organized by stage | After every contact — move rows |
| `crm/CONTACT-LOG.md` | Chronological log of every touch | After every call/DM — same day |

**Where they live**: `matrix_ai/03_SALES/crm/` (Jihad gives you access).

---

## 3. Pipeline Stages (know the emojis)

| Stage | Emoji | Meaning | Your action |
|-------|-------|---------|-------------|
| New | 🔵 | Not contacted | First contact NOW |
| In Conversation | 🟡 | Chatting actively | Keep momentum, book demo |
| Interested | 🟢 | Asked price/demo | Report to Jihad, he closes |
| Stalled | 🔴 | No reply 48h+ | Re-engagement messages |
| Re-demo | 🔵🔄 | Had issues, trying again | Fresh demo, focus on fixing |
| Converted | ✅ | Paid customer | Setup, welcome, check-in |
| Lost | ❌ | 3+ touches no response | Recycle after 30 days |

---

## 4. After Every Touch (60 seconds, do it same day)

**Step 1 — Add a row to CONTACT-LOG (top of table):**

```markdown
| Date | Business | Channel | Type | Message / Summary | Response | Next Action |
|------|----------|---------|------|-------------------|----------|-------------|
| 2026-08-03 | Example Glow BD | Call | Pitch | "Called, introduced MatrixAI, asked about reply time" | "Owner busy, asked to call Sat 11am" | "Call Sat 11:00 — Day-1 script" |
```

**Step 2 — Update the lead's row in PIPELINE.md:**
- Move it to the correct stage section
- Update `Last Touch` (date + what happened) and `Next Action` (specific time + action)

**Step 3 — If you moved a lead to 🟢 or ✅, ping Jihad in the WhatsApp group.**

---

## 5. Lead Ownership Rules (fair play)

- **First toucher owns the lead** — you touch it, it's yours.
- Don't touch leads another agent is working (check CONTACT-LOG first).
- 🟢 HOT leads belong to **Jihad** — report, don't close alone.
- Disputes → Jihad decides. No drama, no poaching.

---

## 6. Follow-Up Cadence (from CADENCE.md — no improvisation)

| Day | Action | Message |
|-----|--------|---------|
| 1 | First contact | Module 3 script |
| 3 | Value share | "৬০% কাস্টমার ৫ মিনিটে রিপ্লাই না পেলে চলে যায়..." |
| 7 | Social proof | BangChar/Monowamart case study |
| 14 | Break-up | "যখন interest হবে, জানাবেন" → Lost |

**Stalled re-engagement** (after 48h): "দেখেছেন? কোনো প্রশ্ন থাকলে বলবেন।" → Day 5 value tip → Day 10 offer → Day 14 → Lost.

**Max 5 touches per lead.** Then Lost. Don't chase ghosts — spend time on new leads.
