# Module 2 — Prospecting

**Goal**: Find the right businesses, skip the wrong ones, and know in 2 minutes whether a business is worth calling.

---

## 1. Who Is Your Customer? (Ideal Profile)

| Criterion | Must Have | Bonus |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Platform | Active Facebook page selling products | Also on Instagram/WhatsApp |
| Size | 5,000+ followers (1K+ can still work) | 10K-50K followers = gold |
| Activity | Posts within the last 7 days | Comments = lots of "মূল্য?" |
| Sales model | Takes orders via Messenger/WhatsApp (not just website) | Delivery all over BD |
| Contact | Page phone number or active Messenger | Email + phone |

**Who to SKIP** (don't waste calls):
- Business centers/offices (no direct seller) — unless they manage many pages
- Pages that haven't posted in 60+ days (dead business)
- Big companies with a full support team (they don't feel the pain — hard sell)
- Pages selling only through Amazon/marketplace apps

## 2. Where to Find Leads (daily habit)

| Source | How | Yield |

**Daily target**: find 5 new businesses, add to pipeline as 🔵 New, contact them the same week.

## 3. The 2-Minute Qualification (before calling)

Ask/check these — if you get 3 YES, call NOW:

| # | Question | Yes means |
|---|----------|-----------|
| 1 | Page posts regularly (last 7 days)? | Active business |
| 2 | Do they reply to messages (comment section shows replies)? | They care about customers |
| 3 | Do they sell products with prices/photos in posts? | Real product business |
| 4 | 5K+ followers? | Volume potential |
| 5 | Are comments full of "মূল্য?", "ডেলিভারি?" questions? | **JACKPOT** — these are missed sales |

**The jackpot example**: a food page where 20+ people commented "মূল্য কত?" and got no reply → call that owner TODAY with this line:
> "আপনার গত পোস্টে ২০ জন দাম জিজ্ঞেস করেছে — কেউ উত্তর পায়নি। আমরা এটা থামাতে পারি।"

---

## 4. Find Their Contact Info (order of attempts)

1. Page "About" section → phone number (most common)
2. Page contact button → mobile number
3. First pinned post → often has WhatsApp number
4. Comment replies → owner sometimes gives number
5. Last resort → Messenger message (takes longer, still works)

**No number found?** Send the Day-1 FB DM (`module-03`). If no reply in 48h, skip — list is big, don't fall in love with one lead.

## 6. Pipeline Math (know your numbers)

| Metric | Per Day | Per Week |
|--------|---------|----------|
| New leads found | 5 | 25 |
| Touches (call/DM) | 5 | 25 |
| Conversations started | 2 | 10 |
| Demos booked | 1 | 5 |
| Closed customers | — | 2 (target) |

**Rule of thumb**: 25 touches → 10 conversations → 3 demos → 1 close. If you're not closing, you're not touching enough.
